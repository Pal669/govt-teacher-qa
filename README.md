## Eligibility & Documents

### What do I need to become a Delhi Government PGT (Economics) teacher?
*Asked 2026-10-01*

You need two things in hand by the **closing date** of a DSSSB ad (eligibility is checked on that date, not on exam day):

- **Qualification:** Master's in Economics / Applied / Business Economics with 50%+ aggregate, AND a B.Ed (or B.A.B.Ed / 3-year B.Ed-M.Ed) from an NCTE-recognised institution.
- **Age:** not over 30. OBC (Delhi) gets +3 years (33), SC/ST +5.
Under the current rules there is no option to join first and finish the B.Ed later.

Pay: Level 8, Rs 47,600 – 1,51,100, Group B. Exam: one tier, 300 marks, 0.25 negative marking.

### Is my OBC certificate valid for DSSSB?
*Asked 2026-10-01*

**Not as it is, but it's fixable.**

- It's issued by a Delhi Deputy Commissioner's office, but in the **central format** ("for appointment to posts under the Government of India"). DSSSB needs an **OBC (Delhi)** certificate.
- The Delhi High Court ruled in July 2025 (GNCTD v. Nisha) that a certificate meant for central govt posts can't be used for Delhi govt posts.
- Your community is on Delhi's own OBC list, so you qualify. Apply for an OBC (Delhi) certificate at your SDM office / Delhi e-District.
- The non-creamy layer (NCL) part must be recent and issued before the ad's closing date.
- Keep the central certificate: it's the right one for KVS and EMRS (with a fresh central NCL).
Without a valid OBC (Delhi) certificate, DSSSB treats you as General, with an age limit of 30.

### What does 'first division' mean, and does it matter for me?
*Asked 2026-10-01*

**First division = 60% or more aggregate** in that exam (second: roughly 45-59%, third: roughly 33-44%; exact cut-offs vary by board). If you got a CGPA, use your university's conversion formula.

Under the **old** Delhi PGT rules (used until early 2024), a candidate with first division in Class 12, graduation AND PG could join **without a B.Ed** and finish it within 3 years of joining.

That clause is gone from the current rules. It would matter again only if Delhi restores the old rules, or for NDMC posts if NDMC still uses its old rules (to verify).

### What are the different ways I can become a government PGT Economics teacher?
*Asked 2026-09-29*

Your Master's in Economics covers the subject. What every route still needs is a **B.Ed** from an NCTE-recognised college (simple: a teaching degree approved by India's teacher-education regulator). CTET is not needed for PGT; it only covers classes 1-8.

The recruitment routes:

- **DSSSB PGT Economics:** Delhi govt schools (Directorate of Education, plus NDMC). Delhi-only posting. Best fit for staying in Delhi.
- **KVS PGT:** Kendriya Vidyalayas (central govt). Transferable anywhere in India. Recent drives run jointly with NVS through CBSE.
- **NVS PGT:** Navodaya Vidyalayas, residential, mostly rural campuses. Hardest on lifestyle.
- **EMRS PGT:** Eklavya Model Residential Schools (NESTS, Ministry of Tribal Affairs), tribal areas across India.
- **State recruitment** (e.g. Uttarakhand UKPSC, Himachal HPRCA): state schools, posting within that state. See Other Routes.
Pay for PGT is Level 8 everywhere: basic Rs 47,600 rising to Rs 1,51,100.

B.Ed options: regular 2-year B.Ed (entrance tests like DU B.Ed, IPU CET). A 1-year B.Ed for postgraduates was announced by NCTE but is not confirmed as started. IGNOU distance B.Ed needs 2 years of teaching experience first, so it does not work for you now.

Rough timeline without a B.Ed: 1-2 years for B.Ed, then the next notification, then a 6-12 month selection cycle, so about **2.5-3.5 years** to actually get posted.

**Correction (checked 2026-10-01):** the "36" age limit for DSSSB is NOT notified. Every official DSSSB PGT ad through Oct 2025 says not over 30 (OBC Delhi 33). See the Age Limits section.

### What has the PGT age limit been since 2005? Was 36 the old norm, and are people fighting the change?
*Asked 2026-10-01*

**Yes: 36 was the long-standing rule, it was cut to 30 in 2022, and candidates are still pushing back.**

- **1975 - 2012:** 36 (per news sources; no DSSSB ads online from this period).
- **2014, 2017, 2020:** official ads say 36. The 2014 ad includes PGT Economics.
- **Jul 2022:** most PGTs still 36; English and Computer Science already 30.
- **29 Sep 2022:** new Delhi DoE PGT rules cut it to **30**. Protests followed in Oct 2022, and a CAT case was filed in 2023. No reversal.
- **2023 - Feb 2024:** a mix of 36 and 30, depending on when each post was requisitioned. The last 36 was PGT Economics in Advt. 07/2023.
- **Dec 2024 onward:** 30 everywhere.
- **Mar 2026:** the minister says 36 is being 'considered'. **May 2026:** new Fine Arts rules still say 30.

### What is the meaning of Shift 1 and Shift 2?
*Asked 2026-10-01*

A shift is a time slot on exam day. When too many candidates apply for one sitting, DSSSB runs the exam more than once that day:

- **Shift 1:** morning, usually around 9 am-12 noon.
- **Shift 2:** afternoon, usually around 2-5 pm.
You get one shift, printed on your admit card; you don't choose it. **Each shift has a different question paper** (same syllabus, pattern and difficulty), so morning candidates can't leak questions.

**Normalisation (score adjustment):** with several shifts, DSSSB adjusts marks so someone with a slightly harder paper isn't disadvantaged.

For practice, any shift's paper is equally useful. Male and Female PGT Economics are separate posts, so their exams are sometimes on different days.

## Other Routes (KVS, EMRS, Other States)

### Is KVS a good backup if Delhi govt doesn't work out?
*Asked 2026-10-01*

**Yes, mainly because of age room.**

- **Age:** 40, +3 for OBC = 43. Years more than DSSSB's 33.
- **Needs:** Master's 50%+ and a B.Ed. Uses the **central** OBC certificate with a fresh NCL.
- **Last cycle:** combined KVS + NVS recruitment run by CBSE, notified Nov 2025. Two tiers (Tier I 11 Jan 2026, Tier II 29 Mar 2026). From aggregator sites; check the official ad.
- **Catch:** ads come every 2-3 years, and the post is transferable all-India.

### What is EMRS PGT, and does it fit me?
*Asked 2026-10-01*

**EMRS = Eklavya Model Residential Schools.** Central govt boarding schools for ST students (Classes 6-12), mostly in tribal districts. Recruiter: NESTS (Ministry of Tribal Affairs); exam run by CBSE.

- **Posting:** anywhere in India, residential duties; requests to change station are not entertained.
- **ESSE-2025:** 155 PGT Economics posts. Tier I qualifying; merit on Tier II (Economics) only.

## Pay & Perks

### How do the perks, benefits and holidays compare: DSSSB (Delhi govt) vs KVS?
*Asked 2026-10-01*

**Same pay. The differences are location, medical cover, your kids' schooling and workload.**

- **Pay:** both Level 8 (Rs 47,600 - 1,51,100); same DA.
- **HRA:** DSSSB is always the Delhi rate (highest). KVS depends on your city.
- **Pension:** both NPS, no old pension. UPS exists for central staff; whether it covers KVS or Delhi govt is unverified.
- **Medical:** Delhi govt has DGEHS; KVS has **CGHS** in big cities (extended July 2024).
- **Kids' school:** KVS staff children are admitted to any KV at any time, even if the class is full.
- **Summer 2026:** DoE 11 May - 30 Jun (with remedial-class duty 11-23 May); KVS Delhi region 8 May - 16 Jun.
- **Winter:** DoE about 1-15 Jan; KVS 23 Dec - 11 Jan.
- **Workload:** DoE has more outside duties (election/BLO, surveys) and big classes; KVS is more structured.
- **Location:** DSSSB is Delhi for life; KVS can post you anywhere in India.
Items marked unverified in the source file are standard govt rules still to be confirmed.

### What pay will I get as a Delhi PGT when I join, and how much will it grow year on year?
*Asked 2026-09-29*

**Joining salary, Pay Level 8 (Delhi rates, my calculation, not an official figure):**

- Basic pay: Rs 47,600
- DA at 60% (rate since Jan 2026): Rs 28,560
- HRA at 30% (Delhi): Rs 14,280
- Transport allowance (Rs 3,600 + DA): Rs 5,760
- **Gross: ~Rs 96,200 a month.** Less pension contribution (10% of basic + DA) Rs 7,616, so **take-home ~Rs 88,000**.
Under the new tax regime, income tax should come to roughly zero at this level (my calculation). Coaching sites quote Rs 65-82k take-home, but they use older DA and HRA rates.

**Growth:**

- **Annual increment:** 3% of basic every year (1 Jan or 1 July). Basic reaches roughly Rs 62,000 by year 10.
- **DA:** raised twice a year with inflation, usually 2-4 percentage points each time.
- **8th Pay Commission:** set up Nov 2025, report expected around mid-2027. Estimates of the jump are 17-23%, nothing final.
- **MACP (guaranteed upgrades without promotion):** move to a higher pay level at 10, 20 and 30 years of service if not promoted (central rule; Delhi generally follows it).
**Pension:** new joiners are on NPS (market-linked). The central govt added UPS (50% of last basic after 25 years) from April 2025.

## Lifestyle & Posting

### If I'm posted to a KVS school outside Delhi, how will I commute? Will I rent a flat, or does the government provide accommodation?
*Asked 2026-10-02*

**You'd live in that town, not commute from Delhi. Either you get a staff quarter, or you rent locally and draw HRA.**

- **Staff quarters:** KVS gives govt accommodation wherever the school has its own quarters, or where the 'sponsoring agency' (the defence, PSU or govt body the KV was opened for) provides them. KVs in cantonments, PSU townships and campuses often have them.
- **Not guaranteed:** the govt told Parliament KVS builds a small set of quarters per school and has no plan to house 100% of staff. KVS had built about 14,700 quarters by 2014.
- **Allotment:** under the KVS (Allotment of Residence) Rules, 1998, by your pay level. You pay a small licence fee and **lose your HRA** while in the quarter. If you refuse an allotted quarter, HRA can be stopped.
- **If there's no quarter:** you rent a flat or room near the school and get **HRA** of 30% / 20% / 10% of basic for X / Y / Z-class cities. At the Level 8 entry basic of Rs 47,600, that's about Rs 14,280 / 9,520 / 4,760 a month.
- **Commute:** there's no transport allowance for living far away. Normal central-govt transport allowance applies, and teachers usually live close to the school.
- **Transfer costs:** when you're transferred, central rules cover moving expenses (transfer TA and a lump-sum grant). Standard rule; to verify for KVS.
Tip: in small towns the HRA is low, so a staff quarter is often the better deal. Ask about quarters when you report.

### In KVS, how often will I be transferred? Can I stay in Delhi for life once I get transferred there?
*Asked 2026-10-01*

**There's no automatic rotation, but there's no lifetime Delhi guarantee either.**

- You move only when you request it, when you're displaced, or when KVS needs to fill a vacancy.
- **First posting** can be anywhere. Direct recruits serve 5 years at a normal station before requesting a move.
- **Hard / North-East stations:** 3-year tenure, and **+30 points** towards your next request. The best route back to Delhi.
- **Displacement:** after 5+ years at one station with a displacement count of 10+, you can be moved out to make room for people returning from hard stations.
- Men under 40 who haven't served a hard-station tenure can be moved to fill vacancies.
- Delhi counts as one 'station', so moving between Delhi KVs is not a transfer.
Realistic path: first posting outside Delhi, then request Delhi, then likely many years there, with some displacement risk.

### How many holidays and leaves does a Delhi PGT get? Is there a two-month summer vacation?
*Asked 2026-09-29*

**Yes, about 7 weeks off in summer.**

- **Summer vacation 2026:** 11 May - 30 June (51 days). Teachers usually report back 2-3 days early.
- **Autumn break:** about 3 days. **Winter break:** about 1-15 January.
- **Public holidays:** Delhi's official list, plus 2 optional holidays and up to 5 special school holidays.
Catch: principals can call 2-3 teachers in during summer for admissions or office work. If called in, you get earned leave in exchange.

**Leave:**

- Casual leave: 8 days a year (+2 optional holidays)
- Earned leave: **none in a year you take the full vacation** (only if vacation is cut short). This is the trade-off for long breaks.
- Half-pay leave: 20 days per year of service (can become 10 full-pay days for medical reasons)
- Child care leave: 730 days over the career (women, and single fathers)
- Paternity leave: 15 days. Special casual leave: up to 15 days a year for set purposes.

### Where will I be posted in Delhi, what is the actual work, what are the hours, and are Saturday-Sunday off?
*Asked 2026-09-29*

**Posting:**

- About 1,050 Delhi govt schools plus 37 specialised-excellence schools (2023-24); about 1,250 counting govt-aided.
- The Directorate of Education decides after selection: **anywhere in Delhi, Narela to Najafgarh**. You don't pick. Never outside Delhi.
- Transfers: apply online once a year, after 2 years in a school.
- **Double-shift schools:** girls in the morning (~7 am-12:30 pm), boys in the evening (~1-6:30 pm). As a man you'd often teach the evening boys' shift.
**The work:**

- Economics for Class 11-12 plus CBSE board prep; sometimes lower classes when short-staffed.
- Class teacher duties, PTMs (big ones on Saturdays), admissions, board invigilation and copy checking, trainings, data entry.

**Hours:** about a 6-6.5 hour school day plus some prep at home. **Sundays off. Saturdays usually off but not guaranteed** (PTMs, remedial classes, trainings; the law needs 220 working days).

**Work-life balance, honestly:** very good among govt jobs (long holidays, short fixed day, security, no targets). Downsides: paperwork, extra duties, possible evening shift, long commute to a school you didn't choose.

## Career Growth

### What are the career prospects after becoming a PGT?
*Asked 2026-09-29*

- **PGT:** Level 8 (Rs 47,600 basic), enter via DSSSB.
- **Vice Principal:** Level 10 (Rs 56,100). Half by promotion, half by direct UPSC recruitment.
- **Principal:** Level 12 (Rs 78,800). By promotion or UPSC direct; about 10 years' experience needed.
- **Beyond:** Education Officer / Deputy Director of Education, or teacher-training posts at SCERT and DIETs.
**The opening:** 828 principal and vice-principal posts are vacant in Delhi, and UPSC is recruiting 124 principals and 704 vice-principals directly. The realistic faster route is PGT, then the UPSC vice-principal exam, then principal. Otherwise promotion is mostly by seniority and can take 10-15 years.

**Limit:** no way to earn more by performing better; pay follows the pay table, and paid side work is restricted (see Service Rules).

## Service Rules & Side Work

### As a PGT, can I publish research papers and give my opinion on government policies?
*Asked 2026-09-30*

**Research: yes. Criticising government policy: no.** Delhi govt teachers come under the CCS (Conduct) Rules, 1964 (simple: the code of conduct for central and Delhi govt employees).

**Allowed:**

- Research papers in journals, with a note that the views are your own and no official information used.
- Books and explainers (occasional literary/academic work needs no prior sanction if teaching doesn't suffer; royalty may need declaring).
- Neutral explainers ("how the repo rate works"); seminars and guest lectures (take permission for paid or organised ones).
**Not allowed:**

- **Rule 9:** any statement with the effect of adverse criticism of current or recent government policy, in print, TV, online or speech.
- **Rule 8:** running or editing a newspaper, periodical or media channel needs prior sanction; a regular YouTube channel or newsletter can fall under this.
- **Rule 15:** paid advisory, AUM fees, RIA work, monetised content without sanction. In practice the advisory book can't continue.
- **Rule 16:** frequent buying and selling of securities (F&O, intraday) counts as speculation and is prohibited. Long-term investing is fine.
- **Rule 5:** no political participation.
**For you:** the job would take away your public policy voice, your advisory book and option trading while in service. Neutral research and data explainers survive.
